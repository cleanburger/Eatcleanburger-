Our Promise

WHY CLEAN BURGER?

Every ingredient is chosen intentionally. No shortcuts. No compromises.

🐄

Grass Fed Beef

Made with clean, high-quality ingredients you can feel good about. Packed with real flavor and made the right way.

🌿

Non GMO

Every ingredient we use is non-genetically modified. We believe in food the way nature intended — pure and unaltered.

📋

Simple Ingredients

We keep our ingredient list short on purpose. If you can't pronounce it, we don't use it. No fillers, no mystery additives.

🥑

Avocado Oil

We cook with premium avocado oil — a heart-healthy fat with a high smoke point that brings out rich, clean flavor in every bite.

🚫

No HFCS

Zero high fructose corn syrup — ever. Our buns, sauces, and condiments are all free from this hidden sugar culprit.

💪

High Protein

Our burgers are naturally high in protein — built for people who care about performance, recovery, and fueling their body right.

🌱

No Antibiotics

Made with clean, high-quality ingredients you can feel good about. Because what goes into the animal eventually goes into you.
